# Privacy Policy for Event Manager

**Effective Date:** August 31, 2025

## 1. Information We Collect
Event Manager stores all data locally on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or store any personal information on external servers.

### Data Stored Locally:
- Event/party names and codes
- Guest names and payment status
- QR codes generated for guests
- Event statistics

## 2. How We Use Information
All data is used solely for:
- Managing your events and guest lists
- Generating QR codes for entry tracking
- Displaying event statistics
- Providing app functionality

## 3. Data Storage
- All data is stored locally on your device using AsyncStorage
- No data is transmitted to external servers
- No data is shared with third parties
- No analytics or tracking services are used

## 4. Camera Permission
The app requests camera permission only for:
- Scanning QR codes for guest entry
- This permission is only used when you actively scan QR codes
- No photos or videos are stored or transmitted

## 5. Data Security
- Data is stored securely on your device
- No internet connection is required for core functionality
- You maintain full control over your data

## 6. Data Deletion
- You can delete individual guests or entire events within the app
- Uninstalling the app removes all stored data
- No data recovery is possible once deleted

## 7. Children's Privacy
This app does not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

## 8. Changes to Privacy Policy
We may update this privacy policy. Users will be notified of any material changes.

## 9. Contact
For questions about this privacy policy, contact: fabriziosanino@example.com
